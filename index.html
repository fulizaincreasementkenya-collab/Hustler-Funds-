Key features of the personal loan product.

KSh 100 – 50,000

The published personal loan range starts at KSh 100 and is capped at KSh 50,000, subject to eligibility and scoring.

⚡

Digital access

Designed around digital access through supported mobile-money channels.

🔐

No collateral

The personal loan is designed as an unsecured digital credit product.

📅

14-day repayment

The published personal loan product provides a 14-day repayment period.

💰

5% savings

Five percent of the approved amount is allocated to savings while the balance is disbursed.

Build your record

Repayment history can influence future borrowing eligibility and limits.
